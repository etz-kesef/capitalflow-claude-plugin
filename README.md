# CapitalFlow for Claude

Read-only access to [CapitalFlow](https://capitalflow.co.il), a Tel Aviv Stock Exchange research site: securities, prices, indices, MAYA filings and report summaries, screener, and your own watchlist and holdings.

## Setup

1. Install the plugin from the directory.
2. Claude opens a CapitalFlow sign-in page. Sign in and approve the connection.
3. Ask, for example: "What did Bezeq report last quarter?" or "Which stocks on my watchlist moved most today?"

Access is by invitation. If the sign-in page says your account has no permission, ask CapitalFlow to enable it.

## What it can and cannot do

- Read only. It cannot change your account, lists or anything else.
- It sees the site's public data and your own watchlist and holdings. Never other users' data, passwords or contact details.
- You can cancel the connection at any time through CapitalFlow.
- Requests are rate limited.

## Notes

- Report summaries are generated automatically and can contain errors. Check material figures against the original filing.
- Information only, not investment advice.

## Privacy Policy

Full policy: https://capitalflow.co.il/privacy?lang=en In short: the assistant receives only what the tools return, which goes to Anthropic under its own terms; CapitalFlow logs the read requests made in your name, does not sell personal data, and deletes account data on request.
